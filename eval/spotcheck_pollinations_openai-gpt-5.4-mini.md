## ✅ adm-4: What scholarships are available for B.Tech students?
*route: retrieval · cost: 0.00449 · 7.0 s*

For **B.Tech students at IIT Ropar**, the main scholarships/freeships mentioned in the official sources are: [1][4]

- **Institute Merit-cum-Means (MCM) Scholarship**: available to undergraduate engineering students, including 4-year B.Tech. students at joining. It is for about **25% of students**, gives **Rs. 1000 per month** to general category students, and includes **tuition fee exemption**. For continuation, the source says the student must have **CGPA ≥ 6.0**, **SGPA ≥ 6.0** in the previous semester, and **earned credits not less than 20 times the number of semesters registered**. [1]
- **Institute Merit Prizes and Certificates**: for the **top 7%** of students in each 4-year B.Tech. programme in the 1st and 2nd semester. The prize value is **Rs. 2500**. Students with **SGPA/CGPA below 8.50** are not considered. [1]
- **JEE Advanced Rank Merit Scholarship**: for B.Tech. students with good JEE Advanced rank. The handbook says:
  - **Rank 1–500**: scholarship equal to **full tuition fees**
  - **Rank 501–1000**: scholarship equal to **half tuition fees**
  Continuation requires **CGPA ≥ 7.0**. [4]
- **National Scholarships Portal / government and NGO scholarships**: students can apply for scholarships through **https://scholarships.gov.in**; the handbook also mentions state/central government, NGO, and other external agency scholarships. [1][4]
- **Top Class Education Scholarship for SC/ST students**: listed on the scholarships page as a Government of India scholarship that SC/ST students can opt for via the National Scholarships Portal. [1]

A few additional notes from the sources:
- The scholarship page also mentions **other external scholarships** from NGOs and agencies, and says **B.Tech.-M.Tech. dual degree** students with good academic performance may get a scholarship in the final year, but that is not a B.Tech.-only scheme. [1]
- The handbook is for **AY 2025–26**, so please confirm details like eligibility and continuation on the official site before applying. [4]

## ✅ hos-1: What are the hostel rules?
*route: retrieval · cost: 0.00475 · 14.1 s*

Here’s a concise summary of the IIT Ropar hostel rules for AY 2025–26. The official hostel rules page links to the hostel rules document and penalty schedule, and the rules PDF says these regulations are effective from AY 2025–26. [1][3]

### Main hostel rules
- **Maintain discipline and decorum** in the hostel. [3]
- **Semester vacation rule:** all students **except Ph.D. and M.Tech. students** must vacate the hostel during semester breaks as per the academic calendar. [3]
- **Special stay exceptions:** students like **Preparatory Course / B.Tech. / M.Sc. / Dual Degree / B.Sc.-B.Ed.** are generally not allowed hostel stay during vacations, unless permitted on request and with competent approval for valid academic/work reasons. [3]
- **Use hostel furniture properly**; the hostel provides basic furniture such as a cot, almirah, study table, and chair per student. [3]
- **Inform the hostel office immediately** if your status changes during your stay. [3]
- If the hostel management finds a student **not eligible** for hostel accommodation, the student must **vacate immediately**. [3]
- If you want to be exempted from hostel stay, you must apply **separately every semester** through the Academic Section to the Dean/Associate Dean/Hostel Management/Student Affairs, and approval depends on the genuineness of the case. [3]
- **Drinking, smoking, and tobacco use are strictly prohibited** in hostels. [3]
- **Senior batch students are not allowed** to enter first-year hostels/wings. [3]
- **Inflammable items are not allowed** in the hostel. [3]
- **Mess registration is mandatory** for every hosteller in the allotted mess. [3]
- **Pets/dogs/cats are not allowed** in hostel premises. [2]
- **Outdoor games** like cricket and football are prohibited inside hostels, but **table tennis, carrom, and badminton** may be played only in designated areas. [3]
- Hostel authorities may conduct **spot checks without prior notice**; students must cooperate. [3]
- Students must carry their **valid institute ID card at all times**. [3]
- Students should **not change rooms without prior approval** from the warden/associate warden in charge. [3]
- Students are responsible for **locking doors and safeguarding their belongings**; the hostel management is not responsible for personal property loss/damage. [3]
- Any **damage to hostel property** must be reported immediately, and residents are charged for damages except normal wear and tear. [3]

### Important compliance note
Violations can lead to **penalties, disciplinary action, or even expulsion from the hostel** in serious cases. The hostel management also has a penalty schedule/annexure for indiscipline. [1][3]
