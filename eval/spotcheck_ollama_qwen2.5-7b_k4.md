The hostel rules at IIT Ropar include the following key points:

- **Behavior and Discipline:** Students are expected to maintain acceptable behavior, discipline, and decorum in the hostels.
- **Semester Breaks:** Except for Ph.D. and M.Tech. students, all other students must vacate the hostel accommodation during semester breaks as per the academic calendar.
- **Vacation Periods:** Hostel accommodation is not available to Preparatory Course (PC), B.Tech., M.Sc., Dual Degree, and B.Sc.-B.Ed. students during vacations, and they need to vacate their rooms.
