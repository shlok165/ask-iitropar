## ✅ aca-2: What is the attendance requirement for courses?
*route: retrieval · cost: 0.00000 · 29.7 s*

The attendance requirement for courses at IIT Ropar is a minimum of 75% of the classes actually held. If a student has less than 75% attendance in a course during the semester, in lectures, tutorials, and practicals taken together, the course coordinator may award an 'F' grade in that course irrespective of the student's performance in the tests.

For M.Tech. students, irrespective of the source of assistantship, they must attend at least 75% of classes in each course in which they are registered. If their attendance falls below 75% in any course during a month, they will not be paid assistantship for that month. Further, if their attendance again falls short of 75% in any course in any subsequent month in that semester, their studentship will be terminated.

To get admission to the M.Tech programme at IIT Ropar, you need to follow these steps:

1. **Apply Online**: Apply through the Common Offer Acceptance Portal (COAP).
2. **Meet Eligibility Requirements**: Ensure you meet the Minimum Educational Qualifications (MEQs) and Eligibility Requirements (ERs).

## ✅ hos-1: What are the hostel rules?
*route: retrieval · cost: 0.00000 · 30.2 s*

The hostel rules at IIT Ropar for the Academic Year 2025-2026 include:

- **Behavior and Discipline:** Students are expected to maintain acceptable behavior, discipline, and decorum in the hostels.
- **Vacation Periods:** Students in preparatory courses, B.Tech., M.Sc., Dual Degree, and B.Sc.-B.Ed. programs must vacate the hostels during vacations, though they may be allowed to stay on request with approval from the competent authority.
- **Furniture:** The hostel management provides basic furniture such as one cot, an almirah, a study table, and a chair per room.
- **Room Allocation:** The number of students per room is decided by the hostel management.
- **Status Changes:** Students must inform the warden/associate warden/hostel office immediately if their status changes during their stay.
- **Eligibility:** The hostel management can ask students to vacate if they are found not eligible for hostel accommodation.
- **Exemptions:** Students can apply for exemption from hostel stay, but it depends on the circumstances.
- **Prohibited Activities:** Drinking, smoking, and use of tobacco products are strictly prohibited in the hostels.
- **Damage and Reporting:** Any damage to hostel property must be reported immediately to the warden/caretaker.
- **Playing Games:** Outdoor games like cricket, soccer, etc., are prohibited inside the hostels, but indoor games like table tennis, carom, and badminton can be played in designated areas.
- **Spot Checks:** The hostel authorities can conduct spot checks without prior notice.
- **Identity Cards:** Residents must carry their valid Institute-issued identity cards at all times.
- **Room Changes:** The hostel authorities can move residents to other rooms/hostel units if necessary.
- **Safety:** Residents are advised to lock all doors for safety before leaving their rooms.

These rules are effective from the academic year 2025-2026. Please confirm on the official site for the latest updates. [1]
